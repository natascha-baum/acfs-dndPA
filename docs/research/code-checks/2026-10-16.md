## Findings
1. **`PlayerSheetCard` is complex and untested** (`src/features/player/PlayerSheetCard.tsx:20`). A change to one section can break another without anyone noticing. *Fix:* section components + one render test.
2. **Long editors** (`NpcEditor` 241 lines, `PlayerEditor` 200). *Fix:* move each fieldset into its own component in `src/components/editor/`, as already started with `fields.tsx`.
3. **`generateNpc` complexity 39** (`src/features/npc/generateNpc.ts:131`). Well tested, so low risk. *Fix (later):* extract `rollAbilityScores`, `pickProficiencies`, `pickEquipment`, `pickSpells`.
4. **`useAsync` in `lib/`** breaks the layering rule. *Fix:* move it to `src/hooks/useAsync.ts`.
5. **Shared card CSS** duplicated between NPC and scene cards. *Fix:* a shared `card.module.css`, or accept it as deliberate.

## Recommended next steps
